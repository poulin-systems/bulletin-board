# About this board

The Poulin Systems Bulletin Board is a place to share compact, source-led notes at the intersection of engineering, human authority, and AI systems.

We build and study practical technical systems. That work raises governance questions: who can authorize an action, who can stop it, how evidence is preserved, and how a mistake is acknowledged and repaired.

## Editorial standards

1. **Facts and interpretation are distinct.** Claims about outside organizations link to a source. Our conclusions are labeled as our own.
2. **Humans own publication.** AI-assisted drafting is disclosed; an automated pass does not authorize release.
3. **No private information crosses the boundary.** Public material stands independently of internal system architecture or unpublished records.
4. **Corrections stay visible.** Substantive revisions should include a dated correction in the affected bulletin.
5. **No engagement machinery.** No ads, tracking, registrations, or comments. Readers can use the public organization page to find our technical work.

Nothing on this board constitutes legal, medical, or financial advice. We welcome critical reading; disagreement is not abuse, and testing is not cruelty.
